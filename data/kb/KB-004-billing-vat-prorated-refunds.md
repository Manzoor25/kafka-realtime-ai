# KB-004: EU VAT Reverse Charge, Invoice Corrections, and Prorated Refunds

## Retroactive EU VAT ID Application
Business customers in European Union member states who forgot to enter a valid VIES VAT registration number prior to invoice generation may request a **VAT Reverse-Charge Credit Note** within **30 calendar days** of the invoice date:
1. Validate the VAT number in **Settings -> Billing -> Tax Identifiers**.
2. Submit the invoice number (`INV-YYYY-NNNN`) to Billing Support; a credit note and re-issued zero-VAT invoice are issued within **2 business days**.

## Plan Downgrades & Prorated Refunds
- Mid-cycle seat reductions generate an automatic **prorated account credit** calculated to the exact minute remaining in the billing period.
- Cash refunds back to the original payment method are eligible within **14 days** of initial annual subscription purchase or when duplicate charges occur.
